# Skill scoring

Verified evidence is weighted by source: instructor-reviewed project or verified assessment `1.0`, accepted challenge `0.9`, milestone `0.4`, and lesson completion `0.2`. An assessment score is preferred when available; otherwise the weighted evidence is normalized only when at least one reliable item exists. Confidence is high at three or more evidence items and weighted evidence of at least 2.5, medium at two items, and `not_yet_assessed` otherwise.

The score is explainable and includes evidence count, confidence, last assessed time, and a plain-language explanation. It is not a certificate, professional credential, or automatic reward.
