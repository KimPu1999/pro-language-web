# Content review workflow

A draft revision is submitted to a review request. The requester cannot approve their own revision. Decisions bind to the revision ID, expire after the configured review window, and are audited. Approval is required before publishing.

## Phase 33 marketplace resources

Resource states are draft, submitted, approved, changes requested, rejected,
published, unpublished, and archived. Human moderation decisions are stored in
marketplace review requests, decisions, and audit logs. AI content assistance
is not a publication authority.
