# Phase 34 instructor academy audit

Existing instructor roles, course builder/publishing, certificates, community,
mentorship, marketplace resources, and platform RBAC were inspected. Phase 34
adds an evidence layer for onboarding, training, quality reviews, peer reviews,
development, certification eligibility, and appeals. Existing course and
certificate records remain authoritative.

Instructor role activation is approval-only. Training and quality data are
empty until persisted records exist; no performance scores or completion are
fabricated. AI coaching is draft-only and provider-gated.
